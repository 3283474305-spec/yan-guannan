01 — About

A climate for
ideas.

Currently: building small systems that reward attention.

20.02° N / 110.34° E · Hainan

I'm interested in the moment an interface stops feeling like a tool and starts feeling like a place. That usually comes from restraint: fewer elements, better timing, a surface that responds before it explains.

At BIUH I study how people read screens — what they notice first, what they trust, what makes them want to touch something. Outside class I make small web experiments, collect typefaces and build games that are easy to start and hard to put down.
